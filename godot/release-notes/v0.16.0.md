# Vega finds her voice, pirates change sides, and a stronghold on Vega's Course
- **Vega sounds like herself.** Vega is a woman, but she spoke the same deep lines as every other sailor. She now has her own higher voice for "Ahhh, treasure!", "Aye, captain" and both chest hand-off lines. Everyone else sounds as before.
- **Pirates can change sides.** From Island 8 on, once Vega sails with you, a pirate your crew beats in a sword fight may throw down their sword and join the NAVS instead of being captured. The odds are best when Vega wins the fight (40%), good when she's within 4 squares (25%) and a long shot otherwise (10%). Guards at their posts are half as easy to turn. A pirate who comes over keeps any chest they're holding, fights for you for the rest of the island, and doesn't join your saved crew.
- **Island 8, Vega's Course, is bigger and harder.** It's now a long island against 11 pirates instead of 9, with BlackPatch's stronghold by his ship: a walled fort with one gate, three chests from the raid inside and four guards at the gate (five on Hard, six on Extreme). The pirates leave the hoard where it is, and there aren't enough chests outside the fort to win on their own, so plan a raid.
