# Turn limits for every multiplayer game, a clock you can see; the camera stays put
- **Choose a turn limit for any multiplayer game:** none, 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes, when you set it up: hosting a game, players sharing one computer, or starting a game on a server (where it starts at 3 minutes). When a turn runs out it passes on, and an unanswered fight or hand-off is settled first. Players who join see the limit before they choose their crew, and server lists show it.
- **The turn clock is on screen.** With a turn limit, a clock beside the Menu button shows whose turn it is and the time left: "Your turn 2:41", or "Pirates' turn 0:42" while you wait. It turns gold in the last minute, red in the last 30 seconds, and flashes in the last 10. It stops while the game is paused, and on a shared computer while the hand-over screen is up. On a phone it is a slim strip under the Menu button.
- **The camera no longer follows your crew.** Selecting someone, pressing Tab (Next), or sending someone off leaves the view where you put it. To find whoever is selected, press C (or Centre on a phone): the view jumps to them once.
- Everyone needs 0.14.2 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server (or press **Check for Updates** on the Play on a Server screen).
