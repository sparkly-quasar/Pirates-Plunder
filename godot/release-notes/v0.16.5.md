# Stamina gives a bigger energy tank
- **Sailors with great stamina hold more energy,** so they go further in a turn as well as resting quicker. An average sailor still holds 100; Runners and other sailors with Excellent stamina hold 118, and Vega (stamina 80) holds 130. Coaching stamina raises the tank too. Nobody holds less than before.
- A fight still counts energy only up to 100, so a bigger tank means going further, not hitting harder.
- The readout shows energy out of the sailor's own tank (for example "Energy 130/130").
