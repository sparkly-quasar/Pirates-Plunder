# Campaign islands won on chests; hand a chest over with a double-click
- **Campaign islands can be won on chests again.** On every ordinary island (all but Gallows Rock and BlackPatch's Hideout), getting more than half the chests aboard didn't win it for you, so the only way through was to capture every rival. Now the usual race applies. A game saved in 0.13.0 that you'd already won ends as soon as you continue it.
- **A single click on one of your crew only selects them**, whether they're carrying a chest or not.
- **Double-click a carrier to hand the chest over.** Select who should take it, then double-click the carrier: they walk over and ask for it. With the carrier already selected, double-clicking them hands it to the team-mate right beside them. Double-clicking someone empty-handed still digs. On a phone or tablet, double-tap.
- Everyone needs 0.13.1 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server (or press **Check for Updates** on the Play on a Server screen).
