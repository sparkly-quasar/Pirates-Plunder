# Smugglers' Cove asks for everything, and typing on a phone
- **Smugglers' Cove (campaign island 2) is now all or nothing.** The smugglers only talk once they've nothing left to fight for: get every chest aboard your ship *and* capture every pirate. Capture them all and you carry on alone until the last chest is aboard; get every chest aboard and you still have to round up whoever is left. If a single pirate carrier reaches their ship, the island is theirs. The readout shows chests aboard and pirates still free, and the briefing has a new tip.
- **Typing on a phone or tablet:** the text cursor is thicker, gold and steady (it no longer blinks out of sight), and while you type, a strip across the top of the screen shows what you've written with the cursor in it, so you can see it above the phone's keyboard.
- Plays with 0.15.0: nothing changed in how copies talk to each other.
