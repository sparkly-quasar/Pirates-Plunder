# play on your phone; saved when your connection drops
- **Play on your phone.** The server now hands out the game for web browsers: open `http://<server's address>:24682/` in Safari (with the Tailscale app on, the server's 100.x address) and you're playing on that server, alongside friends on Mac and Windows. Hold the phone sideways.
- **Touch controls.** Tap one of your crew, then where to go; drag to look around; pinch to zoom; double-tap to dig. A bar of big buttons does the rest: Stand, Run, Crawl, Dig, Next, Centre, zoom, Chat and End Turn. Screens are laid out for a phone held sideways and scroll with a finger where they're tall; held upright, the game asks you to turn the phone.
- **Playing alone on a server, your game is saved the moment your connection drops**, and the server is free for others. If your game reconnects (it tries for two minutes), you're put straight back where you were. Otherwise it's waiting for you on Play on a Server, under **Your saved games here: Continue**. Saved games belong to the players who played them, and each player keeps their three most recent per server.
- **With friends, the game pauses for whoever dropped**, counting down two minutes. If they're back in time, play carries on. If not, everyone still playing is asked, and the first answer counts: **give their crew to the computer** (they can take it back when they return), **keep waiting**, or **save the game for later**, which puts it in every player's saved games to continue together.
- Works the same in a friend's hosted game; saving for later there saves to the host's Continue Online Match.
- **Servers:** the package now includes the web version, handed out on TCP port **24682** (`page-port`); the Docker files open it. Saved games are now kept by default (`saves`, one file per game; `save-days` deletes old ones), and `rejoin-seconds` (default 120) is how long a game waits before asking. The old single campaign is kept as one more saved game. See `godot/SERVER.md`.
- Everyone needs 0.10.0 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server.
