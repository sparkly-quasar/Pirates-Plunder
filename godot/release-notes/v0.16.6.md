# Smoother big islands, a clear way to the ship, and sailors who cross sand
- **Much smoother on big islands, above all on phones.** With three or four crews, the game was working out what every sailor could see each time anyone took a step, drawing the island one detail at a time on every frame, and planning every computer sailor at once. Now only a sailor who moves looks again, the island is drawn from a ready-made sheet of squares, and the computer's planning is spread over several frames. On the three-rival campaign island, frames during the computer's turns went from about 0.7 seconds to 0.02.
- **Your crew lands a few squares in from the ship**, leaving the squares round it clear, so they're not in the way when you bring a chest back.
- **Sailors take a short way across sand (or over a hill)** rather than a long walk round on grass. Grass is still preferred when the way round is about as short.
- **A bigger energy tank now helps in a fight too:** Vega fully rested (130) fights 30% harder than at 100.
- **Delete your saved games on a server.** In Play on a Server, each of your saved games now has a **Delete** button beside Continue (press it twice to be sure). If friends played that game too, it only comes off your list and they keep it; when the last of you deletes it, it's gone from the server.
- **When another player leaves or loses their connection, you're told at once and asked what to do:** carry on with the computer sailing their crew, wait for them, end the game (nothing is kept), or save it for later. The game stays paused for up to two minutes while they might come back, even if they left on purpose (they may have tapped out by mistake). If nobody chooses, the computer takes over after that. They can take their crew back if they return.
- **Dig beside an X that another crew has already emptied, and you're told** ("the Pirates dug its chest up already"), and their flag goes up on it for your side, so you don't waste digs there. (Chests are always on open meadow beside their X; now a test checks that on every kind of island.)
- **A found X's flag sits inside the X's own square**, so it can't be taken for the square next to it.
- **A saved game you reopen waits for the right players.** Each crew is kept for the player who sailed it: nobody else can take it, the computer doesn't fill it, and the game sets sail once they're all back. (The "Set Sail Without Them" button is gone.)
