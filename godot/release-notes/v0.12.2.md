# the whole map on a phone
- **On a phone, the bottom of the island is no longer stuck under the buttons.** The map now scrolls a little past its edges, just far enough that the bottom rows come up above the button bar and the hint line, and the bottom-right corner can be moved out from under the mini-map. The same goes for the top edge and the readout there.
- Zoomed all the way out, the whole island fits between the readout at the top and the buttons at the bottom.
- Dragging past an edge no longer has to be dragged back before the map moves again.
