# Hall of Fame, campaign levels, a new Gallows Rock, and a clearer game
- **Hall of Fame.** Every server keeps a lifetime record for each player who finishes a game there: games, wins, Battle Royale titles, chests aboard and captures. Press **Hall of Fame** beside a server in Play on a Server; your own row is marked.
- **Careers for your crew.** Every sailor in your saved crew keeps lifetime totals (games, wins, chests aboard, captures) over single-player games, the campaign and Battle Royales. They show on **Your Crew**.
- **Campaign levels: Normal, Hard and Extreme**, each with its own progress, all open from the start. Hard: rivals a level harder, with bigger, better-drilled crews. Extreme: the hardest rivals, the biggest crews, and they always move first. Your crew is the same in all three.
- **Gallows Rock, rebuilt.** A long island. Vega's post isn't marked until you scout it; she's held in a stockade with one gate and guarded by BlackPatch's best, and you can't cut her free while a guard stands beside her. Your crew has an outpost on the way where resting gives double energy, and pirates lie in ambush past it. Vega now wears gold.
- **The storm is visible.** Rain falls over Storm Reef and The Long Night, the readout says when your rest is cut, and the start banner explains it. Every campaign island's briefing now has a tip.
- **Crews grow island by island:** you land 6 sailors on the first island and 15 on Treasure Island, with the rival crews growing to match.
- **Recruits always land.** A crew that has grown past an island's number lands whole, and the briefing starts from who's really in your crew.
- **The pirates no longer attack and back away in a loop.** Routes go round opponents you can see (clicking one still attacks), and the computer remembers an opponent's energy once a fight has shown it.
- **A clearer island preview:** rounded coasts, trees, hills and swamp drawn as symbols, bold X's, ships as boats with yours marked, and a key to what each kind of ground does.
- **Landing parties with names and pictures:** under each class, a small figure of it and the names of who would land.
- **New looks:** Runners jog in place, Fighters carry a short sword, Scouts hold their telescope to the eye, and Diggers have a proper spade.
- **Check for Updates on the main menu**, the same as on the server list: it asks before updating.
- The scoreboard says "chests aboard" and "sailors free".
- Everyone needs 0.15.0 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server (or press **Check for Updates**).
