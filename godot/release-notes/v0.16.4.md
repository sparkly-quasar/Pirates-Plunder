# A painted island that's easier to read
- **Woods, thickets, hills and swamp are painted tiles now**, a few of each so the map doesn't repeat. Woods are tall, dark trees; thickets are lower, brighter bushes packed tight (you can't walk through either); hills are rounded knolls with rocks; swamp is mud with pools of water and reeds.
- The tiles were drawn with Google Gemini, from a screenshot of the game.
