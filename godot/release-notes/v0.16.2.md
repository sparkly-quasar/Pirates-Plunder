# A flag on every X whose chest has been found, and Scouts who notice other crews' flags
- **Dig up a chest and your crew plants its flag in the middle of that X**, so you can see at a glance which X's are done. NAVS fly blue, Pirates red, Corsairs gold and Buccaneers green, and a Captain (who has finished the campaign) flies the Golden Rooster.
- **Only a Scout notices another crew's flag.** When one of your Scouts sets eyes on an X where another crew has dug up the chest, their flag appears for your whole side and stays, so you know not to dig there. Your other sailors can walk right past it without noticing. In 2v2, partners see each other's flags and share what their Scouts have spotted.
- Flags are kept in saved games, and nobody's copy is sent a flag their side hasn't seen.
