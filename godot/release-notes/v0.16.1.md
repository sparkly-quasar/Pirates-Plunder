# The campaign lands each island's own number of sailors, and the top line fits on any phone
- **Each campaign island lands its own number of sailors** (6 on the first, 15 on Treasure Island), however big your crew has grown. Starting the campaign over with a crew trained up elsewhere no longer lands everyone on the first island. Vega still comes as well, from island 8.
- **The campaign remembers who sails it.** Whoever landed on the last island lands first on the next, and the rest of your crew waits ashore.
- **When the next island has room for one more**, winning gives you a choice: take on a new recruit, or bring back someone from your crew who isn't sailing yet (or nobody new). On islands that land no more than the last, a win brings coaching only.
- Starting a campaign level over clears who's sailing it, so it begins with six again.
- **On a phone, the line along the top no longer runs under Menu.** What it takes to win (the chests) now comes right after the turn, before the selected sailor's details, and on a narrow screen the line wraps instead of disappearing under Menu. The tutorial panel and the island's opening banner move down to match. On a computer it's as before.
