# Sound on iPhones, and a scoreboard that stays out of the way on phones
- **Sound on iPhones and iPads.** The game's sounds now play in Safari even with the phone's silent switch on, and come back by themselves after a phone call or switching apps (the next tap wakes them). One side effect: like a music app, the game pauses other audio (your music, a podcast) while it plays.
- **On a phone, the scoreboard waits behind a Scoreboard button** beside Menu: tap it to see each crew's chests aboard and sailors free, and tap again (or tap the board) to put it away. With three or four crews it no longer covers the top of the island. On a computer it's as before.
