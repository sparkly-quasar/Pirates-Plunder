# Your crew's names in online games
- **Your sailors keep their names online.** In an ordinary online game (Play Direct or on a server), your crew lands with the names from **Your Crew**, class by class: your Scouts' names go to your Scouts, your Fighters' to your Fighters. Everyone still starts with fresh skills, as before. If another player in the game already has one of those names, or you land more of a class than Your Crew has, the rest get the usual names. A Battle Royale still brings Your Crew whole, skills and all.
- Everyone needs 0.14.1 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server (or press **Check for Updates** on the Play on a Server screen).
