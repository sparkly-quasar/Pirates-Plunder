# The Star Chart campaign, a tutorial, bigger crews, new coaching, save slots
- **Campaign: The Star Chart** (Single Player). Captain BlackPatch's pirates raided the harbour, stole the Star Chart and captured Vega, the fleet's finest navigator. Fifteen islands to get them back, each the same island for everyone, with its own story and rules: storms that cut your rest, pirates who land first, two and three rival crews, and rivals that come better drilled as you go. Your own trained crew sails every island, and you choose the landing party each time: 6 sailors on the first island, growing to 12 on Treasure Island.
- **Rescue Vega.** On island 7, Gallows Rock, she's tied up near the pirates' ship (her spot is marked on the map). Walk one of your crew up beside her to cut her free, then get her aboard your ship. After that she sails with you on every island: a Navigator with skills no recruit starts with, and coaching can take her to 100 in anything.
- **The Star Chart.** On island 12 it's buried under the gold X. Dig it up and get it aboard; if the pirates dig it first, capture the carrier to take it back. With it, later islands start fully mapped.
- **The Golden Rooster.** Win Treasure Island for the ending (your ship sailing home at dawn, with credits for every sailor's record), and you're a Captain: your ship flies the Golden Rooster flag from then on, in single player and in every online game, and server lobbies show you as Captain.
- **Tutorial** (Single Player, replacing Practice Alone). A guided island with a fresh crew of five and one lone pirate: a panel walks you through selecting and moving, standing, running and crawling, ending the turn, digging by an X, handing a chest over, getting it aboard, the menu and a sword fight, and moves on by itself as you do each step. Mouse-and-keys words on a computer, touch words on a phone. It never touches your saved crew.
- **Bigger crews.** Crews are 10 by default (two of each class), up to 16.
- **Coaching, one person at a time.** After a game everyone in your crew gets one try: at a skill, spirit, or an attitude they lack (Coachable, Self-Motivated). Each attitude raises the odds of every later try. Pick for each sailor, or let the game pick the rest, then see how it went. In matches every crew coaches everyone after every game.
- **A recruit after every win** in single player: pick their class (and a name if you like), up to 16 in the crew. Campaign wins bring one too.
- **Go and get the chest.** Select someone with empty hands and click (or tap) a team-mate who's carrying a chest: they walk over and ask, "You want me to carry the treasure?" To select the carrier instead, click them again while the helper is on the way, or press Tab (Next on a phone).
- **Your Crew.** Single Player has a **Your Crew** page: every sailor you've been training, their skills, how far coaching can take them, and their confidence. Rename anyone there; **Start a New Crew** lets them all go (it asks twice).
- **Three save slots.** In a game, **Save Game** (Esc, or Menu) shows three slots; pick one, then keep playing or quit. The main menu's **Continue** picks up the newest save, and **Saved Games** lists all three to continue or delete. Your old saved game is in slot 1. Campaign islands save like any other game.
- Everyone needs 0.13.0 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server (or press **Check for Updates** on the Play on a Server screen).
