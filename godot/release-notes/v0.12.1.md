# the game's icon on your phone's home screen
- **Saved to a phone's home screen, the game now has its own icon and name.** On an iPhone, open the server's page in Safari, then Share → **Add to Home Screen**; on Android, Chrome's menu → **Add to Home screen**. Before, an iPhone showed the icon with black corners around it, and Android had no icon to use at all.
- The loading screen shows the same icon.
- Everyone needs 0.12.1 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server (or press **Check for Updates** on the Play on a Server screen).
