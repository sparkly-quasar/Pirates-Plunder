# a big window on your computer
- **The game opens in a big window.** On a Mac (especially one with a Retina screen) it used to open in a small window you had to enlarge every time. Now the first time it takes most of the screen, and after that it opens the way you left it: the same size and place, or full screen.
- Everyone needs 0.11.1 to play together. Copies from 0.6.1 on update from a friend or a server.
