# Crews land tired, so nobody wins before the other side has moved
- **Every crew comes ashore with half its energy,** and nobody rests until every crew has had its first turn. Until now the crew that moved first landed with a full tank and could often dig up and carry home enough chests to win before a rival had moved at all. A crew of all Runners, with their bigger tanks, nearly always could on a short island. Now the treasure takes a few turns, and the other crews get to contest it.
- From the second round, everyone rests as usual.
- Campaign islands and the tutorial are unchanged: their crews still land fresh.
