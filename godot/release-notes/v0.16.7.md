# Your crew stays your crew, and nobody wins before the other side has moved
- **The sailors you've trained land every time, as they are.** Starting a game against the computer asked for two of each class, whatever your crew was really like. With three Diggers and no Swordsmen, say, a Digger was left ashore and two strangers were made up to be Swordsmen, and they then joined your crew for good. So your crew seemed to change by itself from island to island. Now your saved crew lands with its own mix of classes (topped up to ten with new recruits while it's smaller), and you can still change the mix on the setup screen.
- **A name that can't be used now says why.** Names tell sailors apart between games, so two people in your crew can't share one, and a blank name isn't allowed. That rule includes sailors waiting ashore, who aren't on the coaching screen. Until now the old name just quietly came back. The same goes for naming a new recruit.
- **Every crew comes ashore with half its energy,** and nobody rests until every crew has had its first turn. Until now the crew that moved first landed with a full tank and could often dig up and carry home enough chests to win before a rival had moved at all. A crew of all Runners, with their bigger tanks, nearly always could on a short island. Now the treasure takes a few turns, and the other crews get to contest it.
- From the second round, everyone rests as usual.
- Campaign islands and the tutorial keep their fresh landing.
